Replicave

Replicave.zip · Solo-developed Unity game

Overview

Replicave is a a small rouge-like 2d Top-down shooter prototype that showcases gameplay mechanics, level design, and original assets and free assets. Built from the ground up to explore core loop and feel.

Features

  • Player movement and combat mechanics implemented in Unity.
  • Complex enemy AI behavior and Spawn systems.
  • Prototype art and audio assets integrated.
